# 04 — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

> Primary: Saudi women drivers. Secondary: Saudi male daily commuters.
> Every word on the website must feel like it was written for one specific person.

---

| Gender | Female |

---

## 2. Her Daily Commute Reality

She drives daily. Often alone. Often with children or family. Often in heavy traffic in Riyadh or Jeddah.

Her car is her private space — she cares how it looks and feels.

She manages her phone for navigation, music, WhatsApp while commuting.

She faces three specific daily frictions:

1. **Phone drop** — Her phone slides or falls while she's navigating; she has to reach for it while driving — dangerous, stressful
2. **Seat gap chaos** — Her keys, AirPods, wallet, or phone disappear into the gap between the seat and center console daily — she loses minutes every time
3. **Parking stress** — Parallel parking, tight mall parking, narrow home garage — she feels unsure about blind spots; a scratch on her car is an emotional and financial nightmare

---

## 3. Her Psychographic Profile

### What She Values
- **Safety** — first and non-negotiable; anything that removes distraction while driving has instant value
- **Order and cleanliness** — her car's interior reflects her; a messy car is embarrassing
- **Confidence** — she wants to feel capable and in control; the products help her feel competent, not incompetent
- **Time efficiency** — she does not have time for complex installs or confusing products
- **Premium quality** — she will pay more for something that looks and feels quality; cheap products insult her

### Her Internal Dialogue
When she sees a phone mount:
> "كم مرة وقع جوالي وأنا أقود؟ لو كان عندي شيء يثبته صح، كنت أركز أكثر على الطريق."

When she sees the seat gap organizer:
> "والله هذي المشكلة بالضبط — كل يوم أدور مفاتيحي في نفس الفتحة. أنا محتاجة هذا."

When she sees the parking mirror kit:
> "الاصطفاف في موقف المول يوتري — لو أشوف أكثر، أصير أحسن."

### What Makes Her Buy
1. She **sees herself** in the problem described — immediate recognition
2. She **understands the mechanism** — not magic, not hype; it makes sense physically
3. She **trusts the brand** — it feels Saudi, it speaks her language, it looks premium
4. **Zero financial risk** — COD means she can review the product before paying
5. **Easy, fast decision** — simple form, 2 fields, done in 60 seconds

### What Stops Her From Buying
1. Looks cheap or low quality
2. Website feels foreign / not designed for her
3. Online payment required — she doesn't trust random websites with her card
4. Confusing process or too many steps
5. Can't tell if it fits her car model
6. Fake-looking reviews or exaggerated claims

## 6. Her Buying Journey (Step by Step)

```
1. AWARENESS
   ↓ Sees TikTok/Instagram video showing the problem she recognizes
   ↓ Hook: "إذا وقع جوالك مرة وأنت تقودين، هذي لك"

2. CONSIDERATION
   ↓ Clicks to product page
   ↓ Reads headline — immediately describes her specific pain
   ↓ Watches short demo video — sees the mechanism working
   ↓ Reads "How it works" section — understands WHY it works
   ↓ Reads 2-3 reviews from Saudi women — social proof from her peers

3. TRUST BUILD
   ↓ Sees COD badge — financial risk eliminated
   ↓ Sees delivery timeline — knows when it arrives
   ↓ Sees returns policy — safety net confirmed
   ↓ Sees material specs — feels it's real, not cheap

4. DECISION
   ↓ Clicks "اشتري الآن"
   ↓ Cart drawer opens — sees cross-sell suggestion
   ↓ Decides to add (or not) — no pressure
   ↓ "إتمام الطلب"

5. CHECKOUT
   ↓ Popup opens — just Name + Phone
   ↓ No card details required — relief
   ↓ Confirms order in <60 seconds

6. POST-PURCHASE
   ↓ Upsell offer appears — 12 seconds, low pressure
   ↓ Thank you page — order confirmed, delivery explained
   ↓ Feels confident: "أنا عارفة وش يصير الحين"
```

## 7. Objections & How to Handle Them

| Objection | Response Strategy |
|-----------|------------------|
| "هل يناسب سيارتي؟" | Include compatibility note / "يناسب معظم سيارات السيدان والـ SUV" |
| "الكمية قليلة؟" | Scarcity framing: "الكميات محدودة هذا الأسبوع" |
| "مو متأكدة من الجودة" | Material specs + "راجعيه قبل ما تدفعين" |
| "هل التوصيل سريع؟" | Show city-specific delivery ETA |
| "كيف أرجع المنتج؟" | Short, clear returns policy (7 days, easy process) |
| "هل الدفع آمن؟" | "الدفع عند الاستلام فقط — بدون دفع إلكتروني" |
| "غالي شوي" | Value framing: "مشكلة تتكرر كل يوم تستاهل حل واحد فقط" |

## 8. Copy Voice When Addressing Her

### Do
- Speak to her directly: "أنتِ" (feminine second person)
- Acknowledge her reality: "نعرف إن يومك مليان"
- Give her logic, not just emotion: "هذا يعمل لأن..."
- Treat her as intelligent: no condescension
- Validate her pain before offering the solution

### Don't
- Use masculine default pronouns (إنت, هو)
- Talk down to her ("هذا سهل جداً حتى للمبتدئين")
- Exaggerate or promise miracles
- Make her feel like she has a problem to be fixed — she's solving an inconvenience, not fixing herself
